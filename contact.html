FAQS

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

How far in advance should I order?

1-3 days before, same day order may subject to flowers availability.

Can I customise my bouquet?

Yes, definitely can. Just send in your budget, theme colour, type of flower.

Can I request a specific delivery time?

Yes, can. You may inform us early and we will let the runners know.

Can I include a personalised message card?

Yes, can. Printed card message is BND2 (no word limit). Handwritten card is Free Of Charge.

What is your cancellation or refund policy?

• Please contact Fatin’s Florette as soon as possible if you wish to cancel your order. • Cancellations made before flower preparation begins may be considered for a refund, subject to any costs already incurred. • Once flower preparation or customisation has begun, cancellations may not be eligible for a full refund.

Important Notes

• Refunds are not guaranteed for changes of mind, incorrect order details supplied by customers, or natural variations in fresh flowers. • This policy does not limit any consumer rights that may apply under Brunei Darussalam law.
